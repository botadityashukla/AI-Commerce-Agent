FAQ Knowledge Base

1. Refund Policies
- We offer a full refund within 30 days of purchase if you are unsatisfied.
- To request a refund, go to Settings > Billing > Request Refund.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
- Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded, unless defective.

2. Payment Failures
- Ensure your card has sufficient funds and is not expired.
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal.
- If your payment fails, try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser.
- Error code 402 means the bank declined the transaction. Contact your bank.

3. Account Issues
- Keep your password secure. We will never ask for your password via email.
- To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
- If your account is locked, wait 30 minutes and try again.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled in Security settings.

4. Shipping Delays
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.
- Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
- Weather conditions and holidays may cause delays.
- You can track your order using the tracking link passed in your confirmation email.
- If your package is lost (no update for 7 days), contact support immediately.

5. Subscription Cancellation
- You can cancel your subscription at any time from the Dashboard.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
- We do not offer prorated refunds for partial months.
- To reactivate, simply log in and select a plan.

6. Contact Policies
- Support hours are Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST.
- Email support is available at support@example.com.
- For urgent issues, use the live chat on our website.
- We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours.
